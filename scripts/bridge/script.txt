桥接展示

统一入口：

  bash scripts/demo.sh <command>

配电站 policy + ROS bridge：

  HEADLESS=false bash scripts/demo.sh policy-substation

MoveIt Servo：

  bash scripts/demo.sh servo

桥接结构：
  ROS /cmd_vel -> UDP :15000 -> Isaac policy
  Isaac GT odom/joint_states -> UDP :15001 -> ROS /odom /joint_states /tf /clock
  MoveIt Servo trajectory -> UDP :15003 -> Isaac right arm override

实现细节在：
  scripts/_internal/bridge/ros2_g1_udp_bridge.py
  scripts/_internal/bridge/play_g1_policy_cmdvel_udp.py
  scripts/_internal/bridge/moveit_servo_udp_bridge.py
